Why isn't there an Apple login button?

Real Sign in with Apple requires a registered Apple Developer Services ID, domain, return URL, and secure server. It would identify you to Snap Ledger, not automatically grant access to Marvel Snap. We won't imitate Apple's sign-in screen or ask for game tokens.
